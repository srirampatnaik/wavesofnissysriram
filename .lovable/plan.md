
# 💕 Valentine's Day Website for Nissy

A deeply emotional, beautifully designed single-page love letter website from Sriram to Nissy.

## Design Theme
- **Colors**: Soft pink, rose, blush, and pastel romantic palette
- **Typography**: Elegant cursive font (Google Fonts - Playfair Display + Great Vibes) paired with clean modern text
- **Animations**: Floating hearts, gentle fade-ins on scroll, soft hover effects on photos
- **Overall Feel**: Like opening a handwritten love letter — intimate, warm, and premium

## Sections

### 1. Hero Section
- Full-screen romantic hero with the beach photo (looking at ocean together) as background with a dreamy overlay
- Large cursive title: *"To My Forever, Nissy ❤️"*
- Subtitle: *"Our love began in Vizag, and my heart has belonged to you ever since."*
- Floating hearts animation throughout
- Smooth scroll-down indicator

### 2. Our Story Section
- Emotional timeline-style narrative telling the journey:
  - Meeting in Vizag by the beach
  - Long beach rides and endless laughter
  - Sharing meals and dreaming of new places
  - Nissy standing by Sriram during his hardest times
  - Growing stronger together every day
- The beach selfie photo placed alongside the story
- Gentle fade-in animations as user scrolls

### 3. Memories Gallery
- Beautiful photo gallery showcasing all 4 uploaded photos
- Scrapbook-style layout with soft rounded frames and subtle shadows
- Gentle hover zoom/glow effects
- Romantic captions under each photo (e.g., "Where it all began", "Us, always")

### 4. Reasons I Love You
- 10 romantic reasons displayed as elegant cards with heart icons
- Includes: "You stood by me when I had no one", "You are my peace", "You are my happiness", "You are my strength", "You believe in me", "You understand me", "You support my dreams", "You make me smile", "You make life beautiful", "You will always be in my heart"
- Staggered fade-in animation

### 5. Special Message / Love Letter
- Styled like a handwritten letter on parchment/paper texture
- Emotional message from Sriram expressing deep gratitude, unconditional love, respect, and a promise to cherish every moment
- Acknowledges that no matter what the future holds, Nissy will always have a permanent place in his heart
- Signed "Forever yours, Sriram"

### 6. Final Section
- Big emotional closing with animated hearts
- *"No matter where life takes us, my heart will always remember you, Nissy. 💕"*
- *"Happy Valentine's Day, My Love ❤️"*
- Soft pulsing heart animation as the grand finale

## Technical Details
- Fully mobile responsive — designed to look stunning on phone (since she'll likely open it there)
- Smooth scrolling between sections
- All 4 photos embedded meaningfully across sections
- No backend needed — purely frontend
- CSS animations for floating hearts and scroll reveals
